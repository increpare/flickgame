# App Store text to translate

Flickgame is a small, minimalist iOS app (used by kids and adults) for making simple point-and-click games:
a game is 16 pictures, and tapping a colour in one picture jumps to another picture.

Translate two things per language.

## 1. description

Flickgame is a tiny tool for making games out of pictures.

A game is 16 pictures. Draw them with your finger. Then pick a colour in one picture and choose which picture it leads to. When someone plays your game and taps that colour, they jump there.

That's all there is to it, and you can make a lot with it: stories, mazes, puzzles, jokes, a tour of your house.

• Draw with 16 colours, with lots of palettes to choose from
• Link any colour to any picture
• Try your game without leaving the editor
• Your games are kept in a gallery on your device
• Send a game to a friend as a file. It opens in Flickgame on their phone or tablet
• No accounts, no ads, nothing uploaded anywhere

An example game is included to get you started.

There's an optional one-time supporter purchase if you'd like to say thanks. It doesn't unlock anything.
