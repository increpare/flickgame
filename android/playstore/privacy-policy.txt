Flickgame for Android — Privacy Policy

Flickgame is made by Stephen Lavelle. Contact: analytic@gmail.com.

Your games and drawings stay in the gallery on your device. Flickgame does not
create an account, upload your work, show advertisements or collect app analytics.
The editor, gallery, player, examples and help work offline. The app's WebView cannot
load websites or connect to outside servers, and the app has no internet permission.

When you choose a game using the system file picker or open one from another app,
Flickgame reads that file to import it. When you share a game, Android gives the app
you choose temporary read access to that exported file. The recipient app's own
privacy policy applies to what it does with your file.

Games are stored in app data. Automatic cloud backup and Android device transfer
are disabled. Uninstalling Flickgame or clearing its app data deletes the gallery.
Exported files remain wherever you chose to keep them. Export your games to make
backups; temporary copies used for sharing are cleaned up on a later launch after
24 hours.

There is an optional, one-time supporter purchase through Google Play. It changes
only the supporter smiley and does not unlock features. Google Play processes the
payment according to Google's policies; Flickgame does not receive your payment
card details. The app checks and stores the purchase entitlement locally. Google Play's purchase service may
use the network even though the game tools do not.
