Flickgame is a drawing tool for making simple picture-based games. A game is 16
pictures; a colour in one picture can lead to another picture.

No sign-in is needed. All games stay on the device. There is no in-app online gallery,
no uploads, no ads and no app analytics. Only the optional Google Play purchase
uses Google's purchase service; WebView traffic is blocked outside bundled assets.

On first launch the gallery includes icecream, heart and cat. Tap a play triangle to
play, or the paintbrush to edit. In the player, drag to move the pointer and tap to
press; a hand means that spot links to another picture. In the editor, draw with a
finger and tap a colour twice to choose which picture it leads to. The grid button
switches between the 16 pictures. The menu contains Save, Help and Back to Gallery.

The folder button opens Android's document picker. Games are exported through the
Android share sheet as .flickgame files. They are compatible with Flickgame on iOS;
renaming to .html makes a standalone game for a browser. Opening/sharing one from
another app imports it and opens the player. No broad storage permission is needed.

Supporter (com.increpare.flickgame.supporter) is an optional one-time non-consumable.
Tap the smiley at the top left of the gallery to buy it. It changes only the smiley
and unlocks no features. Restore purchase is at the bottom of Help and also happens
automatically when the app resumes.
